Task 19 – Top 10 Products by Sales

Objective

Identify the top 10 products based on total sales using Microsoft Excel.

Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

Tool Used

- Microsoft Excel

Analysis Approach

1. Used the Product Name and Sales fields from the dataset.
2. Created a PivotTable to calculate total sales for each product.
3. Sorted the total sales from Largest to Smallest.
4. Identified the first 10 products as the Top 10 Products.
5. Checked the 10th and 11th positions for a possible tie.
6. Created a Top 10 Products table.
7. Created a bar chart to visualize the sales performance of the top 10 products.

Key Outcome

The analysis identified the 10 products with the highest total sales in the Superstore dataset.

Deliverables

- Top 10 Products table
- Excel PivotTable analysis
- Top 10 Products chart
- PDF report
- Rollback evidence

Rollback

The original raw data was preserved without modification. The analysis was performed separately, allowing the Task 19 analysis sheet and chart to be removed without affecting the original dataset.

Conclusion

The Top 10 Products analysis provides a clear view of the products generating the highest sales and demonstrates the use of PivotTables, sorting, ranking, and visualization in Excel.
